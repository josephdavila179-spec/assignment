How to Embed a YouTube Video

Learn how to add a video to your website in a few simple steps.

Introduction

Embedding a YouTube video allows visitors to watch a video directly on your website without having to leave the page. Follow the steps below to learn how.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Find a YouTube Video

Open YouTube and search for a video that you would like to add to your website.

Step 2: Open the Video

Click on the video you want to use. Make sure the video is appropriate for your website and allows embedding.

Step 3: Click the Share Button

Under the video, click the Share button to open the sharing options.

Step 4: Select Embed

In the sharing menu, select the Embed option. This will open a window containing the HTML code for the video.

Step 5: Copy the Embed Code

Click the Copy button to copy the iframe code provided by YouTube.

Step 6: Paste the Code into Your HTML

Open your HTML file and paste the iframe code wherever you want the video to appear on your webpage. 

<iframe
    width="560"
    height="315"
    src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/aqz-KE-bpKQ"
    title="YouTube video player"
    frameborder="0"
    allowfullscreen>
</iframe>

Step 7: Save and Preview Your Website

Save your HTML file and open it in a web browser. You should now see the video displayed on your webpage.
